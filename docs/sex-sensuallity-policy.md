# Sexuality and Sensuality Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the permitted level, narrative function, and progression of sexual and sensual content in the series. It applies to series architecture, episode planning, prose generation, revision, and audio production.

The intended content level is **steamy, cutting at the threshold of sustained graphic sexual description**, while deliberately using the upper end of that range.

The objective is not to minimize sexuality. Sexual attraction and intimacy are important components of the romance and may carry significant character, emotional, and plot development. The distinction is between **high sensual intensity** and **graphic anatomical or mechanical description**.

## 1. Content Standard

The series may depict sexual attraction and consensual adult intimacy directly rather than automatically fading to black.

Permitted on-page and on-audio material includes:

- strong sexual tension and expressed desire;
- charged proximity, anticipation, and almost-contact;
- kissing and escalating physical intimacy;
- undressing;
- intimate touching;
- sexual desire expressed through dialogue or internal thought;
- characters initiating or consenting to sex;
- waking or lying together;
- physical and emotional aftermath;
- bite, mark, scent, bond, and other supernatural intimacy imagery;
- vulnerability, conflict, reconciliation, or revelation occurring during intimate encounters; and
- consequences of sexual or romantic intimacy for the characters, relationships, supernatural bonds, and political circumstances.

When a scene reaches **sustained graphic description of the sexual act itself**, the narration should transition through that portion of the encounter rather than describe each physical action in anatomical or mechanical detail.

The governing distinction is therefore:

> **Restrain graphic description, not intimacy.**

## 2. No Automatic Fade-to-Black Rule

This policy does not require a scene to end merely because characters undress or begin having sex.

An intimate scene may contain a complete dramatic progression, including:

**anticipation → initiation → escalation → emotional turn → transition → aftermath**

Important dialogue, decisions, revelations, reversals, supernatural developments, or changes in a relationship may occur during an intimate scene. Such material should not be artificially relocated merely to avoid depicting sexuality.

When explicit physical detail is elided, the **dramatic continuity of the scene should remain intact**.

The audience should understand what occurred, why it mattered, and what changed because of it.

## 3. Narrative Function

Sexual and sensual material must serve the same narrative standards as every other scene. Intimacy should accomplish one or more meaningful functions, such as advancing a relationship, revealing character, changing trust or vulnerability, creating or resolving conflict, affecting supernatural or political circumstances, or paying off previously established romantic tension.

Intimate scenes should not exist solely to satisfy a presumed episode quota.

Likewise, the series should not avoid an intimate scene when intimacy is the natural dramatic consequence of the relationship at that point in the story.

## 4. Escalation Across the Series

Romantic and sexual intensity should operate as a progression rather than a binary switch.

Early episodes should preserve the value of proximity, anticipation, interrupted contact, restraint, scent, touch, private conversation, and almost-crossed boundaries. Later stages may progressively introduce greater physical and emotional intimacy as the relationship earns them.

Consummation is not the end of romantic escalation. Subsequent development should come from changing vulnerability, trust, knowledge, emotional stakes, supernatural consequences, and the meaning of intimacy between the characters rather than simply increasing graphic detail.

Accordingly, the series should **not spend its strongest intimacy cards during the acquisition runway merely to manufacture an early hook**.

## 5. Consent and Supernatural Influence

Supernatural attraction, mate-bonds, heightened desire, instinct, blood effects, or similar mechanisms may intensify attraction or create conflict. They do not constitute consent.

Where supernatural influence affects desire, the distinction between **involuntary response** and **voluntary choice** must remain narratively legible.

Consent to intimacy must come from the characters, not from the supernatural mechanism acting upon them.

This distinction is especially important when intimacy intersects with bonding, claiming, vows, political obligations, unequal power, or supernatural compulsion.

## 6. Audio Performance Standard

The series is written for spoken performance by a narrator. Every intimate passage must therefore pass a **read-aloud test**.

Sensual, emotionally intense, and provocative language is appropriate. Language that becomes awkwardly anatomical, clinical, mechanically descriptive, or unintentionally comic when spoken aloud should be rewritten, compressed, implied, or elided.

The target is language a narrator can perform convincingly as part of a dramatic scene without the prose suddenly changing registers.

## 7. Platform Boundary

Pocket FM carries adult programming described as containing sexually explicit material, including supernatural and dark-romance content. Sex and adult intimacy should therefore not be treated as categorically prohibited.

However, no sufficiently specific public author guideline has been identified that establishes a reliable boundary for the degree of graphic sexual description permitted.

The series must therefore **not be architected around discovering, exploiting, or repeatedly testing that boundary**.

If platform-specific requirements become available, those requirements supersede this section and this policy should be updated accordingly.

## 8. Governing Rule

When deciding whether an intimate passage belongs in the series, apply this test:

> **Keep the desire, intimacy, vulnerability, choice, emotional turn, and consequences. Reduce or transition through sustained graphic anatomical or mechanical description.**

The desired result is an adult romance that can become intensely sensual without confusing explicitness with emotional intensity.
