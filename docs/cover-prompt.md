## THE WINNING PROMPT — 989 characters (attempt 2, approved)

Dark romantasy audiobook cover, vertical. Two beautiful people almost kissing — and one of them is in chains.

HER, front: breathtaking, late twenties, pale skin, dark hair to her jaw, blazing dark eyes staring straight at the viewer. Black coat, chin lifted. Untouched. In control.

HIM, at her shoulder: gorgeous and dangerous, dark hair, silver eyes, black suit, looking down at her, wrecked with wanting.

THE HOOK: a glowing red-gold chain bursts from his chest, coils around his throat and wrist, and ends wrapped around HER closed fist. He is bound. She is not. Embers where it burns him.

Enormous blood-red moon, steel bridge girders, a huge grey wolf at her side.

Amber rim light, cold blue moonlight, saturated, deep blacks, high contrast, glossy.

TITLE across the lower third, exactly: THE HALF-SPOKEN VOW
Ornate 3D gothic serif, calligraphic flourishes, beveled silver, engraved shading, warm inner glow, heavy drop shadow. Crisp and legible, hyphen between HALF and SPOKEN.

### Why this one is different

The first two prompts described a *scene*. This one describes the *premise*: he is bound and she is
not. The chain running from his chest to her closed fist is the series in a single image — he spoke
half a vow, it bound only him, and he handed her the dangerous end. Everything else on the cover is
shelf convention (blood moon, wolf, glamour, ornate metal title); the chain is the only thing on that
storefront nobody else has.

If the model struggles with the chain, the fallback in priority order is: chain visible on *him* only,
even if it does not reach her hand. The asymmetry matters more than the connection.

## WHY ATTEMPT 1 FAILED

- Described a documentary *scene* rather than a cover. Produced a wide conference-room shot with three small figures and no focal point.
- Canon-accurate physical description ("not a fashion model", "minimal makeup", "faint tiredness") produced an unflattering lead. The shelf is beauty-forward without exception.
- "Realistic film still, prestige contemporary drama poster, no plastic digital smoothness" pushed it toward flat, cold, unsaturated realism. Competing covers are glossy, warm, and high-contrast.
- 3,600 characters diluted the signal. Comparable covers need ~1,000.
- Title placement assumed top; the generator places it bottom.
