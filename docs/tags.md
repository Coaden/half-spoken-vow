## DELIBERATELY OMITTED — do not add these

These are the highest-traffic tags in the werewolf-romance category and every one of them is a lie
about this series. Mis-tagged listeners bounce, and **listener retention is a judged contest criterion**,
so a tag that pulls the wrong audience actively costs points.

- **alpha, alpha male, luna, omega, beta, pack alpha** — this series has no Alpha/Omega hierarchy at all. Its wolves are a chartered mutual-aid local with an elected business agent. A listener arriving for Alpha/Luna dynamics will leave in episode three.
- **moon goddess, prophecy, chosen one, ancient prophecy** — none exist. The moon here is an ordinary dated calendar event with an economic cost.
- **mating bite, marking, claiming bite, heat cycle** — not our mechanics. The bond completes by a spoken vow before a witness.

- **secret princess, hidden royalty, lost heir, secret bloodline** — her hidden identity is an erased line on a union roster, not a throne.
- **dark romance, dubcon, mafia, billionaire** — wrong content and wrong promise. Our consent architecture is the opposite of dubcon.
- **shifter harem, reverse harem, polyamory** — single central pairing.
- **teen, young adult, high school** — the protagonist is thirty-one and worried about rent.
