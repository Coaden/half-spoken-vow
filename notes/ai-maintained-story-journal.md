## 2026-08-22 — The Sixth Named: Kovach, A.

**Source:** `Assets/Book One/Episodes/B1E09-Seven-Names.md`

### What is established in the manuscript

- The Sewickley house ledger lists **“Item the sixth. Kovach, A. Nineteen ninety-two.”**
- The entry has neither a clause-number disposition nor the word **Released**.
- Nadia Kovach is the human, third-generation steward of the Sewickley house.
- The initial matters: **Kovach, A. is not Nadia Kovach.**

### Reader-facing effect

The entry deliberately invites the question of whether the sixth named woman was related to Nadia, and
what happened to her. The missing disposition is a strong unresolved-record hook.

### Current continuity status

The manuscript, episode map, Codex, and ledger do **not** currently identify A. Kovach or schedule a
payoff. The shared surname is therefore a promising but untracked promise, not a locked reveal. Do not
write as though Nadia’s relationship to A. Kovach is known until Troy chooses it.

### Recommended option for author consideration

Make **A. Kovach Nadia’s mother**. She was named in 1992 and disappeared from the official record without
satisfaction or release. Nadia remembers the consequence and preserves the illicit house ledger partly
because the official record reduced her mother—and every other named woman—to a disposition.

This would:

- give Nadia a personal, non-villainous reason to protect the house’s other record;
- make her cautious help to June emotionally grounded rather than merely professional;
- make the blank disposition a natural future entry point into record tampering and the unreported
  severance thread; and
- preserve Nadia as an ally with her own history and limits.

### Decision still required

Before this can be locked, decide:

1. A. Kovach’s full name, background, and relationship to Nadia.
2. Why she was eligible to be named under the Articles while Nadia is canonically human.
3. Whether her disappearance is tied directly to the unreported severance mystery, or merely exposes a
   related historical abuse.
4. The future book and episode where June learns the answer.

**Status:** Open author decision. No current manuscript change authorized.
