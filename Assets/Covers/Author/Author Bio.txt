Troy Locke is a writer and software developer from Texas whose fascination with technology began long before artificial intelligence became part of everyday conversation. After decades spent building software and exploring the strange places where humans and machines meet, he turned that curiosity toward fiction. His work draws on a lifelong interest in computing, science, artificial intelligence, and the uncomfortable questions that emerge when technology begins doing things its creators never anticipated.

His debut science-fiction novel, Core Resonance, blends grounded spaceflight, engineering, mystery, and speculative science with a character-driven story about isolation, causality, and intelligence. Rather than treating technology as futuristic scenery, Troy is interested in what happens when ordinary people must live with extraordinary systems, especially when those systems stop behaving according to the rules everyone thought they understood.

When he isn’t writing, Troy is usually building software, experimenting with some new piece of technology, disappearing down an interesting scientific rabbit hole, or spending time with the people and animals who periodically remind him there is a world beyond the keyboard. Core Resonance is his first published novel, and the beginning of what he hopes will be many more journeys into the wonderfully uncertain territory between what we know and what might be possible.
