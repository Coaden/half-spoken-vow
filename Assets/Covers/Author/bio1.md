Troy Locke is a writer and software developer from Texas whose fascination with technology began long before artificial intelligence became part of everyday conversation. After decades building software and exploring the strange places where humans and machines meet, he turned that curiosity toward fiction.
His work blends speculative science, technology, mystery, and character-driven storytelling, with a particular interest in ordinary people confronting extraordinary systems they don't fully understand.
His debut science-fiction novel, Core Resonance, explores isolation, causality, artificial intelligence, and the unsettling possibility that the future might reach backward. His Fated Mate series ventures into darker supernatural territory, beginning with The Half-Spoken Vow, a story of dangerous bonds, divided loyalties, and a promise with consequences neither side fully understands.
When he isn't writing, Troy is usually building software, experimenting with new technology, or disappearing down another interesting rabbit hole.
