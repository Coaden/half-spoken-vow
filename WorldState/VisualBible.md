# The Half-Spoken Vow — Visual Bible

**Status:** Lightweight future-facing reference — not a committed deliverable
**Revised:** 2026-08-18 — marketing assets exempted from the restraint rules (see Rules).
**Priority:** Low. Pocket FM audio is the only committed production target; no visual edition is planned.
The character briefs below are locked only in the sense that they mirror `BookCodex.json` visual_identity
fields and must not be contradicted. Do not expand this file speculatively.
**Last updated:** 2026-08-18

## Purpose

This file is the authority for how characters and places look in covers, promotional art, generated
stills, and any later adaptation. The storyboard skill pastes these briefs verbatim into image
prompts, so they are written as prompt-ready sentences rather than as prose description.

## Base style prompt

Append this baseline to every image prompt.

> The Half-Spoken Vow visual baseline: contemporary supernatural romance set in present-day
> Pittsburgh; a working river city of steel bridges, hillside row houses, church basements, wet brick
> streets and downtown law-office glass; materials are worn wood, folding chairs, chipped enamel,
> manila folders, brushed steel and cold river water; lighting is low and practical — overhead
> fluorescents, desk lamps, streetlight sodium, grey overcast daylight, and one warm interior source
> per frame; muted palette of slate, oxblood, brass and river green with skin tones kept warm against
> it; mood is intimate, procedural and quietly ominous rather than gothic or theatrical; realistic
> film still, shallow depth of field, natural imperfection; widescreen sixteen by nine; no lettering,
> no logos, no watermark, no readable text of any kind.

**Never** render this world as castles, fog, candelabra, ballgowns, or ruined abbeys. The gothic here
is institutional: it lives in a document, a folding chair, and a vault at a working cemetery.

## Character briefs

One entry per recurring character. Locked attributes are fixed; anything listed as flexible must not
be silently canonized by a generated image.

### June Havlik

- Locked: thirty-one; five foot four; solid through the shoulders and arms, built like someone who grew up carrying things; dark brown hair, cut by herself, roughly chin length, clipped back off her face; brown eyes; a chipped upper left front tooth, visible only when she genuinely laughs.
- Working wardrobe: dressing to be invisible in a working room — grey and navy layers, plain button shirts, a stretched-out cardigan with a sagging right pocket, flat lace-up shoes, no jewelry. Reading glasses pushed up into her hair. Frequently carrying a stenotype case or a manila folder.
- Physical impression: contained, watchful, still. She holds eye contact a beat longer than is comfortable and does not fidget. Photographs as a competent professional in a bad week, never as a waif or a warrior.
- Flexible until explicitly locked: exact face shape, nose, complexion undertone, tattoos or absence of them, and whether the glasses are permanent.

### Elias Marrow

- Locked: presents as a man of about thirty-four; turned in 1868 at thirty-one; tall and narrow, six foot one; black hair, deliberately unstyled and a little too long at the collar; grey eyes; pale but not bloodless; a puckered burn scar the width of two fingers across the back of his right hand, acquired while alive in a mill.
- Working wardrobe: a good dark three-piece suit of no identifiable decade, worn as a uniform — he owns four identical ones. White shirt, no tie pattern, no pocket square, no watch chain, no rings. A plain dark overcoat. Never period costume, never a cravat, never a long coat with a collar turned up.
- Physical impression: stillness that reads as economy rather than menace; a man conserving something. Photographs like a partner at a small old law firm who has not slept, not like a predator or an aristocrat.
- Flexible until explicitly locked: exact facial structure, hairline, whether he wears glasses for reading, and the visibility of the scar under a cuff.

### Marta Havlik

- Locked: sixty-four; broad and heavy through the shoulders; grey hair in a braid pinned flat against her head; reading glasses on a beaded cord; hands visibly damaged by four decades of manual work, knuckles enlarged.
- Working wardrobe: a Local Nine windbreaker in every weather, over a work shirt; sensible trousers; steel-toed shoes she no longer needs. A clipboard or a bound ledger nearly always in hand.
- Physical impression: immovable and unhurried. Occupies the front of a room without standing at the front of it.
- Flexible until explicitly locked: exact facial features, eye color, jewelry.

### Constance Marrow

- Locked: presents as a woman of about sixty and does so deliberately; turned in 1631 at forty-four; small, five foot two, very upright; silver hair in a low neat knot; half-glasses she does not need; a thin worn gold wedding band from a marriage nobody living remembers.
- Working wardrobe: quiet expensive tailoring in charcoal, dove grey, or ink — soft wool, no pattern, no display of wealth. Low heels. A single strand of small pearls at most. Never black lace, never velvet, never anything that reads as a costume.
- Physical impression: composed, courteous, entirely unthreatening in posture, which is what makes her frightening. Photographs like the chair of a foundation board.
- Flexible until explicitly locked: exact features, eye color, and whether the wedding band is ever shown clearly.

### Cyd Toomey

- Locked: forty-one; short and wide-shouldered, five foot two; home-dyed red hair, roots showing, growing out unevenly; glasses habitually pushed up on top of her head; a chipped enamel mug within reach in almost every scene.
- Working wardrobe: comfortable over presentable — fleece, cardigans over printed shirts, jeans, sneakers. Lanyard with keys. Dressed like a small business owner who stopped trying to impress lawyers years ago.
- Physical impression: kinetic, mid-sentence, mid-task. Always doing two things.
- Flexible until explicitly locked: exact features, eye color, tattoos.

### Tess Renn

- Locked: thirty-eight; lean and wiry, five foot seven; bleached hair grown out several inches dark at the roots, cropped close; a beige hearing aid in the left ear; a canvas work jacket with too many pockets; work boots.
- Working wardrobe: unchanging — the same jacket, the same boots, dark jeans, a plain shirt. Carries a document box or a bulging accordion folder.
- Physical impression: never seated with her back to a room; angled toward exits. Photographs as someone who has been waiting eleven years and has stopped expecting comfort.
- Flexible until explicitly locked: exact features, eye color, visible scars.

### Milo Havlik

- Locked: twenty-four; tall and not filled out, six foot; sandy hair, badly cut; a long jaw that bruises easily; chewed cuticles; a Local Nine jacket two sizes too big because it was his father's.
- Working wardrobe: the jacket over hoodies and work trousers; scuffed sneakers; a phone always in his hand.
- Physical impression: cannot hold still. Photographs as eager and slightly too young for the room he is in.
- Flexible until explicitly locked: exact features, eye color, hair length.

## Place briefs

### the Hall on Cotter Street
A mid-century union lodge: brick exterior with a painted sign, a linoleum entry, a wooden meeting
floor, stacks of grey folding chairs, a raised secretary's table, a coffee urn on a foil-covered
banquet table, a corkboard of notices and a paper moon calendar. Fluorescent overheads. Never mystical.

### the Marrow Trust offices, Fourth Avenue
A small floor in an older downtown building: frosted glass, dark wood casework, brass fixtures,
banker's boxes, an empty reception desk, and heavy interior blinds drawn against daylight in every
room. Grey light bleeding around the edges of the blinds is the signature image.

### June's apartment, Polish Hill
A third-floor walk-up in a narrow row house on a steep street: sloped ceilings, painted-over trim, a
radiator, a fire escape, a card table used as a desk with a stenotype case beside it. Warm lamp light
against a cold window.

### the spans
Any Pittsburgh bridge at night or in rain: riveted steel, sodium lighting, wet deck, black water far
below, the city on both banks. Two figures at a distance from each other, mid-span, is the series'
signature composition.

### Allegheny Cemetery
A working nineteenth-century city cemetery: iron gates, mature trees, hillside paths, Victorian
family vaults in weathered stone. Overcast daylight or flashlight in a stone interior. Not gothic
horror; municipal and genuinely maintained.

### the Sewickley house
A large, beautifully kept riverside house of no recent decade: deep porches, heavy drapes closed on
the sun side, waxed floors, fresh flowers, staff presence implied by order rather than by people. It
should photograph as an extremely comfortable jurisdiction.

## Rules

- Never let a generic image model redefine an established character.
- Never place readable dialogue, titles, episode numbers, logos, or invented interface text inside a generated image.
- Do not add characters, locations, or objects unsupported by the episode text or established canon.
- A generated image that contradicts a locked brief is discarded, not adopted as a new reference.
- **Marketing assets are exempt from the restraint rules.** Covers, thumbnails, and store art exist to earn a click on a shelf where every competing title uses blood moons, wolves, embers, and glamour. Supernatural iconography — a wolf, a red moon, drifting embers, dramatic weather — is permitted and encouraged there, and the leads may be idealized well beyond their canon descriptions. Sales and marketing, not canon illustration.
- **In-story art still holds the line.** Episode stills, storyboards, and any image meant to depict a scene keep the original discipline: no fangs, no claws, no glowing eyes, no mid-transformation states. Inside the story this world's supernatural content is legal and social, and showing monsters misrepresents it.
- The one thing marketing may not do is promise a show we are not making: no Alpha/Luna iconography, no packs of wolves in fur cloaks, no medieval or forest settings. The cover may be glamorous and supernatural; it must still be *contemporary*.
- Do not render intimate content beyond charged proximity and clothed contact, consistent with `docs/sex-sensuallity-policy.md`.
- When a cover or promotional image conflicts with this file, this file wins; note the superseded asset below.

## Asset notes

**Series cover — APPROVED and LOCKED, 2026-08-18.** Generated on Pocket FM from the prompt recorded at
`docs/cover-prompt.md`. It is the visual reference for all future promotional art.

Locked cover attributes, to be matched by any companion asset:

- **Palette:** blood-red moon against deep midnight blue and black, warm amber and ember-orange accents, cold blue fill. High contrast, deep blacks, glossy finish.
- **Composition:** close two-shot, both figures from the chest up, filling the frame. She stands forward and slightly left, looking directly at the viewer. He stands at her right shoulder, taller, looking down at her.
- **Her:** black wool overcoat, dark bob to the jaw, chin lifted, composed and unafraid. Idealized well beyond her canon description under the marketing exemption.
- **Him:** black shirt and black suit, dark hair, pale eyes, contemporary throughout. No period costume, no fur, no ornamentation.
- **World cues:** lit steel suspension bridge, city skyline across water, drifting embers, one large grey wolf standing calm at her flank facing outward — an ally, never a threat, never snarling.
- **Typography:** ornate beveled gothic serif, silver and pale gold, calligraphic flourishes, engraved shading, heavy drop shadow, set across the lower third.

Accepted imperfections, not to be corrected: the bridge resembles the Manhattan Bridge rather than a
Pittsburgh span, and the final word sits close to the right edge.

promotional asset, or published material.
