# The Articles of Agreement — 1871 Draft

**Status:** Clauses One through Three are canon-locked as of 2026-08-21 following the complete Book One
continuity repair. Clauses Four through Nine remain development draft for later volumes. The general
administrative rules used in Book One are canon in substance; this document's period phrasing outside
Clauses One through Three may still be refined without changing those rules.  
**Purpose:** A complete legal-literary draft of the original nine-clause instrument. All nine clauses
are written as having existed at execution in 1871. Later interpretations, constructions, precedents,
depositions, declarations, and execution records may apply or contest this text but may not amend it.

---

## ARTICLES OF AGREEMENT

### Made and Entered in the Year of Our Lord One Thousand Eight Hundred and Seventy-One

### Between Local Nine and the Marrow Trust, for the Keeping of the Peace and the Preservation of Their Several Records

When two Bodies, differing in Blood, Duration, Necessity, and Proof, are nevertheless obliged to inhabit
one Country, cross the same Waters, hold Estates under the same civil Peace, and answer for Injuries which
neither can judge by the Record of the other, Prudence requires that their Concord be neither entrusted
to Affection nor abandoned to Memory.

The one Party bears Truth in the living Body, in Kinship, in Presence, in the witnessed Change, and in the
Voice openly given. The other Party preserves Truth in Writing, in Seal, in Appointment, in Succession,
and in the Instrument which survives the Hand. Neither Party shall be compelled to call the other's
Record sufficient in matters wholly its own; yet each Party, by these presents, acknowledges these
Articles as the place wherein the two Records meet.

Therefore Local Nine, speaking in open assembly by those competent to bear its assent, and the Marrow
Trust, acting by its officers under hand and seal, do establish these Articles as their entire compact;
not for dominion of either over the other, nor for the making of any Person into Estate or Chattel, but
for the certain naming of Duties, the limitation of Remedies, and the continuance of Peace when private
inclination fails.

## Declaration of Reserved Rights

And because a command concealed is but an ambush, and an obligation unnamed cannot be answered, it is
first declared:

1. That no supernatural Duty, Office, Forfeiture, or Claim arising hereunder shall take hold until its
   operative words are named aloud before Witnesses competent to both Records.
2. That a Refusal requires neither writing, witness, seal, cause, nor ceremony; that Silence shall be
   taken for Refusal and never for Assent; and that no Entry may convert Refusal into Speech.
3. That bodily attraction, fear, pain, hunger, change, nearness, direction, or other Symptom shall be
   evidence only of itself, and shall grant no title to the Body, Dwelling, Labor, Affection, or Answer of
   another.
4. That the word **Bride** describes the Person named to the operation of these Articles and creates no
   species, caste, rank, ownership, civil marriage, or lesser capacity.
5. That whatever is required shall be performed as named, and whatever is not named shall not be supplied
   by custom, convenience, office, kinship, implication, or the supposed necessity of Peace.

## Constitution and Operation of These Articles

The foregoing Recitals, the Reserved Rights, and the following Rules of Constitution shall govern every
Clause and every proceeding hereunder. They are not additional Clauses. The operative Sequence consists
of exactly nine Clauses, numbered the First through the Ninth, fixed in that Order and incapable of
acceleration, substitution, enlargement, or rearrangement by any Party acting alone.

### I. Of Naming and the Standing Thereby Created

In each Generation there may be named one living Person as Bride, according to the concurrence of the
written and borne Records. The Naming shall identify the Person by a name sufficient to distinguish her,
shall state the Generation and Line through which she is named, and shall be read aloud before an accepted
Witness while she is present or thereafter personally served upon her.

Naming makes the Person a Party to these Articles with standing to receive Notice, inspect the Record
concerning her, retain Counsel, state Refusal, offer Performance, file Objection, demand Read-Back,
challenge Construction, and seek Satisfaction, Waiver, Release, or Defeat. Naming does not itself:

1. activate any numbered Clause;
2. create Residence, Surety, Custody, Invitation, Office, Membership, civil Marriage, or completed Vow;
3. bind the Bride to the half of a Vow spoken by another;
4. transfer her Body, Labor, Property, Threshold, Kinship, or Affection; or
5. make every Recital or Clause presently due.

The Naming and the numbered Clauses are separate acts in one compact. No Clerk, Reader, Officer, or
Witness shall describe Naming as the performance of the Third Clause or of any other Clause.

### II. Of the Persons and Offices Competent to Act

The following capacities shall be distinct, and no Person shall acquire one merely by performing the
work of another:

1. **The Bride** is the named Party. She may inspect, answer, refuse, perform, file, challenge, and elect
   relief in her own standing. She is not thereby a member of Local Nine, a trustee, an officer, a Surety,
   or property of either Party.
2. **An Authorized Reader** may present a due Clause in the words of the executed text. The Reader may
   explain no omitted obligation into existence and may not become a Witness merely by reading.
3. **An Accepted Witness** is a Person or continuing Office recognized by both Records as competent to
   bear the act in question. The Witness certifies identity, presence, words, capacity, and those
   conditions personally observed; the Witness does not choose the Answer or consequence.
4. **A Recorder or Reporter** may take down, transcribe, compare, and read back what was said. Accurate
   transcription supplies evidence of the proceeding but gives the Recorder no lineage Office and
   creates no obligation beyond the words lawfully named.
5. **A Filing Officer** may receive, number, preserve, and serve an Instrument. Acceptance for filing is
   not acceptance upon the merits and shall not be confused with witnessing a Vow.
6. **Counsel** may advise, draft, examine, object, and argue by ordinary authority. Counsel may not speak
   a personal Answer for the Bride, create a borne fact, or become a trustee by service in daylight.
7. **The Custodial Holder** shall preserve the third Counterpart; present what that Office is required to
   present; convene or certify the joint proceeding when permitted; and preserve every Refusal and
   objection. Custody grants no power to compel Speech or select the consequence.
8. **Local Nine** may bear Kinship, Presence, Change, open Vote, and other bodily facts through competent
   members. It may enforce only those consequences its charter and these Articles recognize.
9. **The Marrow Trust** may prove Writing, Seal, Appointment, Office, Instrument, Estate, and Succession
   through a serving trustee or duly authorized human agent. No agent's service shall silently elevate
   that agent into lineage Office.

No Claim shall be enforced merely because the Person who reads it also keeps the file, or because the
Person who witnesses it desires the result.

### III. Of the Hybrid Record

For Local Nine, a valid Record may be borne in the Body and established in open presence by Kinship,
Scent, witnessed Change, or spoken Vote. For the Marrow Trust, a valid Record may be written, sealed,
filed, registered, or preserved in lawful succession. For purposes of these Articles, a shared Entry
exists only where:

1. the same operative fact is received in both Records; or
2. one Record contains the fact proper to it and the other contains an independent acknowledgment of
   that fact's receipt and limited effect; or
3. the executed Articles expressly designate the Custodial Holder's certification as sufficient pending
   joint review.

One Record may prove what lies within its own competence. It may not lend its conclusion to fill an
element the other Record was required independently to establish. Writing cannot manufacture Kinship.
Borne Kinship cannot appoint an Office. Repetition between them is not independent proof where each
merely repeats the other.

Every Entry shall preserve the exact words spoken, the writing received, the capacity in which each
Person acted, the time, the place, the Witnesses, and every contemporaneous Refusal, qualification, or
objection. An omission shall remain an omission until lawfully supplied; it shall not be made clean by
inference.

### IV. Of Due Date, Activation, and the Fixed Sequence

A Clause becomes **due** only upon the occurrence of the condition stated in the preceding Clause or in
the Schedule executed with these Articles. The existence of a Clause is not its activation. Knowledge of
its words is not Notice. Preparation to answer it is not acceptance.

A due Clause becomes **active** only when an Authorized Reader reads its operative demand, without
material omission or addition, before an accepted Witness, and when the Bride has been given an immediate
opportunity to answer, refuse, request read-back, or object to the Reader's authority and text.

Presentation may be required by either original Institution acting in its proper capacity; by the
Custodial Holder upon independently verified maturity; or by a Claimant whose existing standing has
already been received in both Records and will be directly altered by disposition of the due Clause. A
person possessing information, kinship, grievance, preparation, or an expected benefit but no such
standing may advise or supply evidence to a qualified Claimant, but may not require presentation in that
person's own name. The Reader and Custodial Holder shall verify maturity independently; a qualified
petition requests performance of their Offices and does not delegate those Offices to the petitioner.

No later Clause shall activate while an earlier Clause remains active, suspended, or undisposed, except
where the earlier Clause itself expressly names the later act as its consequence. A Clause defeated or
satisfied shall not revive merely because a later Clause fails.

### V. Of Interruption, Suspension, and Resumption

An active reading may be interrupted by a superior Act recognized in both Records, by joint Order, or by
an impossibility which the Reader and Witness are bound to acknowledge. Interruption shall stop execution
at the word reached and shall preserve everything already spoken without converting preparation into
performance.

The freely spoken first Half of a binding Vow is one such superior Act. It may interrupt an Article
because it places the Speaker personally into both Records and strips the Speaker's protections. It binds
only the Speaker. It shall impose no Vow, Answer, Debt, gratitude, custody, or personal obligation upon
the Bride, and it shall neither complete nor satisfy the interrupted Clause unless that Clause expressly
requires and lawfully receives completed performance.

Unless another term is expressly stated, an interrupted Clause shall remain **suspended** and shall
resume on the thirtieth Day after interruption, at the same operative demand and not at a newly enlarged
construction. Resumption requires personal Notice stating the date, hour, Reader, Witness, text to be
resumed, and every lawful means of Satisfaction, Waiver, Release, or Challenge then available.

Formal abandonment of the interrupting Half shall end that Half and the Speaker's exposure, but only upon
the Speaker's excommunication: the Speaker's name shall be struck from every lineage Office, succession,
institutional protection, and standing to enforce a supernatural Claim. Such excommunication shall be
permanent. It shall not erase the Speaker's ordinary civil identity, divest property held in an
individual civil capacity, sever the involuntary Attachment, erase the historical interruption, speak
for the Bride, or retroactively validate any act taken during suspension. Only the Speaker may perform
abandonment. The Person to whom the Half was spoken has no words to abandon, though an exposed Speaker
remains subject to whatever lawful compulsion the exposure otherwise permits.

### VI. Of Satisfaction, Waiver, Release, Defeat, and Forfeiture

A Clause is **satisfied** only by performance of what its executed words require, entered in both Records
according to the nature of the act. Performance of a later custom shall not be demanded where the
original text permits a narrower act.

A Clause is **waived** only by every Person and Institution whose standing or protection the waived
demand affects, each acting within its own authority after the price is stated. No officer may waive a
bodily right belonging to another Person. Waiver of one demand shall not waive the Sequence or any
unrelated objection.

A Person is **released** where the Institutions relinquish a Claim against that Person and the release is
entered in both Records. Release of a Person is not amendment of a Clause.

A Clause is **defeated** only by the process and grounds stated in the Ninth Clause. Defeat bars execution
but leaves the original words visible. It does not renumber the Articles.

A **forfeiture** arises only where a numbered Clause expressly identifies the Party, standing or Office
forfeited; the act or omission producing it; the exact time of maturity; and what survives the loss. No
forfeiture shall be implied from refusal, silence, delay, kinship, affection, poverty, illness, or the
convenience of either Institution. Refusal survives every forfeiture.

### VII. Of Challenge and Enforcement

The Bride, a Person whose standing is directly altered, either Institution in its proper capacity, the
Custodial Holder, or Counsel acting for such a Party may file a Challenge. A person in custody may
challenge the custody but may not be required to challenge through the Office that holds that person.

A Challenge shall identify the exact executed words, the act complained of, the Record relied upon, the
standing of the challenger, and the relief requested. Filing shall stay new execution only where these
Articles say so, where both Institutions consent, or where the Custodial Holder certifies that proceeding
would make lawful review impossible.

Where a timely Challenge identifies a material difference among the Counterparts in a number, term, or
consequence, execution of the disputed consequence shall be stayed for ten calendar Days. The stay shall
begin at the time the earlier disputed term would otherwise mature, shall neither restart nor amend that
term, and shall preserve all Parties in their pre-forfeiture standing while joint examination proceeds.
At the stay's expiration the common executed text, or failing common text the authenticated original,
shall control unless joint adjudication has already entered another lawful disposition.

No single trustee, member, reader, witness, custodian, clerk, reporter, counsel, or rival claimant may
enforce a disputed supernatural obligation alone. Enforcement requires an active Clause, a competent
Entry, proper standing, and the concurrence prescribed for the fact being enforced. Ordinary civil rights
and remedies remain available without lineage permission.

### VIII. Of Sealing, Inspection, and Construction

All nine Clauses exist from the execution of these Articles. Sealing governs disclosure and custody; it
does not postpone existence, create text, or permit a later officer to write what was absent in 1871.

The Parties may seal a Clause, Schedule, name, commencement condition, or custodial direction until the
time fixed for inspection, provided the person against whom it is to operate receives the complete
operative text before activation and sufficient opportunity to refuse, object, or challenge. A sealed
portion may not execute while withheld from that Person.

A later deposition, statement, declaration, interpretation, construction, precedent, disposition,
margin, or history of performance may show how an executed word was understood or misapplied. It may not
become an amendment by age, repetition, institutional convenience, or the death of those who objected.

Where ambiguity exists, authority shall be considered in this order: the common executed text; a
Schedule executed with it; a contemporaneous definition accepted in both Records; an accepted joint
adjudication; and only thereafter later constructions and histories, which remain contestable. No
construction may contradict the Reserved Rights or supply an obligation the original text did not name.

### IX. Of the Counterparts and Custody Before Activation

The three Counterparts shall be separately kept but shall constitute one Instrument. The Trust shall
preserve one under seal; Local Nine shall preserve one with its witnessed acceptance; and the Custodial
Holder shall preserve the third in civil succession independent of both Parties.

Inspection shall be logged by date, purpose, Persons present, condition of seal, and every page exposed.
No Person who inspects may scrape, overwrite, lift a seal, substitute a page, alter a number, or make a
private correction. A suspected difference shall be photographed or otherwise preserved, read aloud,
and compared without first changing either object.

Nothing in this Section shall be called a tenth Clause. Upon these Reservations and constitutional Rules,
the Parties covenant in the following fixed Sequence.

## Clause the First — Of Residence

**Canon status: Locked.**

The Bride shall **Reside** beneath the roof of the Trust within thirty Days of Naming.

For the purposes of this Clause, to Reside shall bear the meaning held at the execution of these Articles:
to lodge beneath such roof for a stated number of several Nights within the appointed Period, every such
Night to be entered by the Steward of the House and confirmed by one Witness competent to bear the
presence of the Bride. The Bride may tender a stated number; any number accepted in both Records shall
constitute the complete measure of her performance.

Continuous habitation shall not be required. No civil Domicile shall thereby be changed; no Lease,
Dwelling, Threshold, Estate, or former Shelter shall be surrendered; no Invitation shall arise except as
separately and freely given by a Resident; and no officer shall enlarge Residence into custody merely
because custody would make location more certain.

Residence is demanded so that the Bride's location during the stated Nights may exist in both Records.
It conveys no Estate and grants no dominion over her Person.

Upon performance being entered in the written Record and borne by the living Witness, this Clause shall
be satisfied and shall not be twice demanded of the same Bride.

## Clause the Second — Of Surety and the Limitation of Collective Punishment

**Canon status: Locked.**

Upon the satisfaction, waiver, or lawful defeat of the First Clause, the Line from which the Bride is
named shall answer by one fit Person standing as Surety, and not by forfeiture laid indiscriminately upon
the whole Line.

The Line shall bear in open assembly the Kinship and fitness of the Person proposed. The Trust shall
enter the Surety as an Office. The Person so entered shall stand answerable for the Deed, if any, expressly
named against that Office under a succeeding Clause; and while one Surety lawfully stands, no other Person
of the Line shall be charged for the same default.

No presumption of fitness shall establish understanding. No writing of Office shall create bodily
Kinship, and no bearing of Kinship shall, without more, prove acceptance of Office. If the two Records rely
each upon the conclusion of the other and neither contains the Person's own understanding, the Surety
shall be defective and no derivative Claim shall issue from it.

No Surety may be required to speak for the Bride, complete a Vow, surrender another Person's refusal, or
perform a Deed not named before the Office was entered.

## Clause the Third — Of the Term Certain and the Witnessed Vow

**Canon status: Locked.**

When a Surety has been lawfully entered, a Term of nine calendar Days shall commence upon the Bride's
personal hearing of this Clause and the entry of her Answer or Refusal before the Custodial Holder.

Within that Term there shall be entered one of the following: a completed Vow freely and knowingly spoken
in its two several Halves before an accepted Witness; a Release accepted in both Records; or a lawful
Defeat of the demand then executing.

A Vow shall not be complete unless the two Halves are separated by an Interval and unless each Speaker,
without borrowed words, can state these five permanent Consequences:

1. each shall thereafter know the direction and approximate distance of the other;
2. each shall know when the other is dying;
3. the mortal Party shall cease to age yet shall remain wholly capable of being killed;
4. neither shall thereafter form another Attachment; and
5. both shall stand permanently recorded as bonded, with standing that cannot be struck.

Completion shall further end each Party's capacity to swear another binding Oath. It shall guarantee no
love, obedience, fidelity, agreement, allegiance, proximity, shared thought, shared emotion, or happiness.
It shall never be dissolved, severed, annulled, or erased.

No accepted Witness shall certify Completion where either Speaker is compelled, exposed and compellable,
intoxicated, dying, ignorant of permanence, or subject at that moment to a Clause executing against that
Speaker. A true desire expressed under defective Conditions remains a true desire, but it shall not form
the permanent Vow.

If, at Midnight of the ninth Day, neither Completion, Release, nor lawful Defeat has been entered,
the Bride's provisional standing under the pending Sequence shall be forfeited, saving always her bodily
authority to refuse; and the Surety shall pass into custodial keeping solely for the purpose of answering
the next Deed lawfully named. Custody shall not make the Surety property, shall not erase Kinship, and
shall not authorize bodily harm.

No consequence of this Clause shall be represented as consent to the Vow.

## Clause the Fourth — Of Issue After Residence and the Particular Notice

**Canon status: Development draft.**

Upon the entry of Residence and the disposition of the Term Certain, the Trust may **issue** one
Particular Notice concerning any Person, Instrument, Record, Chattel, Appointment, or other Interest
alleged to have entered, departed, been concealed, or been altered under shelter of the Residence.

The Notice shall identify the thing questioned with such particularity as both Records permit; shall
state the act alleged, the Record relied upon, the Person from whom an Answer is demanded, and the Deed,
if any, proposed against a lawfully held Surety. No deficiency shall be created by the Notice itself.
That which neither Record can identify shall remain a Question and shall not become a Debt merely because
an officer has written it down.

The Bride may answer by production, denial, proof that the thing never entered her keeping, proof that it
was lawfully carried away, or proof that the alleged Interest is a Person and therefore incapable of
being disposed of as Property.

No Notice under this Clause shall be served constructively through the other Party to an incomplete Vow.
It shall be served upon the Bride at the Address she has elected for such Notices, with a copy to her
Counsel if named, and a counterpart presented to the Custodial Holder.

Where a Surety remains in custody, no Deed of search, recovery, delivery, or testimony shall be required
until the Notice has been read to that Surety and separately accepted or refused. Refusal may carry a
lawfully stated Consequence; it shall never be rewritten as performance.

## Clause the Fifth — Of Registration, Attachments, and Things Claimed as Property

**Canon status: Development draft.**

Every Person, Instrument, Chattel, Office, Estate, and Interest produced or established upon a Notice
under the Fourth Clause shall be entered according to its true Nature in both Records before either Party
may claim possession, succession, release, severance, or disposition thereof.

A Person shall be registered as a Person and never as Property. An Attachment shall be registered as a
fact between Persons and not presumed to be an Estate merely because it may be written, witnessed, cut,
or made valuable to another. An Office shall be distinguished from its Holder; Kinship from appointment;
Custody from ownership; Possession from title; Shelter from surrender; and bodily condition from assent.

No incomplete Attachment may be sold, assigned, inherited, taxed, severed, or pledged by force of this
Clause alone. Any Party asserting such authority must produce the separate Instrument or witnessed
Commission that creates it, together with the Price named before performance. The absence of a Principal,
Deed, Commission, or Witness shall be preserved as absence and shall not be supplied by inference.

Where the two Records disagree concerning the nature of the thing registered, the disagreement shall be
entered in full. Neither Party may cleanse the conflict by striking the other Record or by appointing a
Person into what the living Body denies.

## Clause the Sixth — Of the Three Appearances and Disposition

**Canon status: Development draft.**

Every disputed Entry remaining after the Fifth Clause shall be heard at three several Appearances, unless
all interested Persons waive an Appearance after its cost is named.

The first Appearance shall be before Local Nine in open presence, where Kinship, fear, illness, recent
violence, and the capacity of the Body to bear the asserted fact may be witnessed.

The second Appearance shall be before the Trust under writing and seal, where title, appointment,
succession, custody, and the continuity of Instruments may be proved.

The third Appearance shall be upon neutral ground before the Custodial Holder, where the two Records shall
be read against one another and where no silent inconsistency shall be treated as agreement.

The schedule shall accommodate the public Nights of Change and the absolute danger of direct Sun. No
Party shall manufacture default by setting an Appearance at a time when the other is physically incapable
of attending; but each may act by the method proper to its Record, provided the Person whose standing is
at issue can hear and answer the substance.

At the third Appearance the interested Person may elect delivery, return, continued custody, release,
separate human adjudication, or preservation of the dispute. No disposition of a Person, Attachment,
Office, or Estate shall be inferred merely from failure to choose among unlawful alternatives.

## Clause the Seventh — Of Remedies, Prices, and Deeds Named

**Canon status: Development draft.**

Where a Clause is lawfully proved and remains unsatisfied, the Remedy shall be a Deed named before
performance and not a resource silently taken afterward.

Every Remedy shall state: the Party bound; the Person or Institution benefited; the act required; the
Record in which performance will be entered; the condition that begins it; the condition that ends it;
and the consequence of refusal. No neighboring Duty shall be enlarged to satisfy another merely because
the same Persons are involved.

Money may settle wages, rent, damage, debt, or any other civil account, but money shall neither create nor
discharge a supernatural Obligation under these Articles. No Receipt shall be made a Vow. No affection
shall be made consideration. No bodily Symptom shall be taken as payment.

No Remedy shall compel either Half of the Vow, manufacture an Invitation, erase Kinship, appoint standing
into a Body, or turn a Person into property. A Deed impossible when named, or a Deed whose lawful
performance would defeat the consent condition it purports to cure, shall be no Remedy.

## Clause the Eighth — Of Offices, Succession, and the Continuance of Custody

**Canon status: Development draft.**

No title arising under these Articles shall be without a Holder, and no Person shall hold two conflicting
Titles at once.

The Trust may appoint succession to an Office by written Instrument, but appointment shall create no
blood Kinship and shall not survive beyond the powers expressly granted. Local Nine may recognize
standing only in open presence according to the Body and its charter; no writing shall manufacture that
recognition. The Custodial Holder shall succeed according to the civil Office in which the third
Counterpart is lodged and shall neither become officer of the Trust nor member of Local Nine by reason of
that custody.

The Bride's personal standing shall not descend, transfer, or become Estate upon death. If the Sequence
ends without Release, Completion, Abandonment, or lawful Defeat, the unresolved history shall remain
visible until the next lawful Naming; but no successor Bride shall be charged with an unnamed Deed of her
predecessor.

An Office may be lost, exchanged, or ended; it shall not be stacked. A Person struck from Office remains
a Person. A Person exiled from membership remains of her or his blood Line. The Record may alter standing;
it shall not claim to alter the body that survives it.

## Clause the Ninth — Of the Joint Peace, the Counterparts, and the Preservation of the Nine

**Canon status: Development draft; its Book One lawful-defeat and severability rules are locked in
substance, while its exact legal wording may be refined before later clauses execute.**

The Parties bind themselves to maintain the Peace secured by these Articles; to recognize lawful Notices,
Refusals, Satisfactions, Waivers, Defeats, Offices, and Successions entered according to both Records; and
to employ no private violence where a remedy is capable of being named and heard.

These Articles consist of nine Clauses, all executed this Day, in three Counterparts: one retained under
the seal of the Trust; one accepted in open assembly by Local Nine; and one lodged with the Custodial
Holder. The three are evidence of one compact and not three instruments capable of separate amendment.

No later Bride, member, trustee, officer, custodian, witness, court, deposition, statement, declaration,
construction, precedent, schedule, marginal note, or history of execution shall add to, subtract from,
reorder, rewrite, or amend these nine Clauses. Such later Records may prove performance, expose error,
distinguish an application, or establish that a demand has been lawfully defeated; they shall never become
new text by repetition.

Where the Counterparts disagree in a word, number, seal, erasure, scraping, insertion, or omission, the
disputed matter shall not alter the shared Obligation. The uncontested common text shall remain in force,
and the disagreement shall be preserved for joint examination. A material dispute timely raised shall
receive the ten-Day stay prescribed above. No Custodian shall treat the altered word as an amendment or
administer it merely because delay is inconvenient.

A Clause may be satisfied, waived by every Party whose standing it affects, or lawfully defeated. A
Clause is lawfully defeated where accepted joint adjudication finds that the demand:

1. requires an act forbidden by another express provision of these Articles;
2. depends upon a fact that neither Record independently contains;
3. has been enlarged by later construction beyond its executed words;
4. cannot be performed without defeating the refusal or consent reserved herein; or
5. no longer serves its stated peacekeeping purpose through any lawful application.

A defeated Clause shall not be erased, renumbered, rewritten, or replaced inside these Articles. Its
words shall remain visible with the judgment entered beneath them, and it shall thereafter be barred from
execution. The operative Count may diminish; the original Number shall remain nine forever.

Any new covenant desired by the Parties shall be a separate Instrument, openly accepted in both Records,
and shall neither revive a defeated Clause nor masquerade as interpretation of this one.

The defeat of one Clause shall not dissolve the remainder where the Peace can continue without the
defective demand. Total invalidation shall require proof that no lawful remainder can perform the compact
and the free election of both Parties after every human and institutional price has been named.

Tampering with a Counterpart, a Register, a witnessed bodily Record, or an accepted judgment shall be an
offense against both Parties. Correction shall be made by annotation and witnessed comparison, never by
erasure.

Thus the Parties declare that Peace shall not depend upon ignorance, that Record shall not be confused
with ownership, that consequence shall not be confused with consent, and that no necessity of either
Line shall furnish words which a Person has refused to speak.

## Drafting Notes — Not Part of the Instrument

1. **Clause One** preserves the operative sentence dramatized in Episode Seven and makes the period
   meaning of *Reside* part of the original instrument rather than a later amendment. The exact count is
   supplied by a tender accepted in both Records; June tenders three consecutive nights, and the Trust
   accepts that measure in Episode Seven.
2. **Clause Two** encodes the precise gap that later practice exploited: the Local bears kinship and the
   Trust enters office. Its own limiting language shows why circular reliance can later be defeated
   without pretending the clause was added after 1871.
3. **Clause Three** uses the sealed original's nine days. The parish counterpart was later altered to
   nineteen. The alteration cannot amend the shared obligation, but the resulting material discrepancy
   triggers the instrument's mandatory ten-day stay when timely challenged: nine days plus the stay
   produces nineteen days in practice. That procedural effect is why a protective custodian could change
   one numeral while understanding that the changed ink itself possessed no amending power.
4. **Clause Four** reconciles “a question of issue” with the later statement that it may issue notice
   concerning completed residence. *Issue* is used in its legal sense: a disputed matter formally put
   forward. The post-residence inventory can lead directly toward the question of what June's mother took.
5. **Clauses Five and Six** reconcile the two existing summaries. Both concern disposition of property:
   Five determines what may be registered as property or standing; Six supplies the institutional
   appearances required before disposition. They also create the arena for severed-attachment trading
   without declaring an attachment property by fiat.
6. **Clauses Seven through Nine** follow the later Episode Fifty-Eight summary: remedies, succession, and
   joint peace. Clause Nine supplies the proposed original lawful-defeat and severability authority needed
   by the Book One verdict while making amendment impossible.
7. This draft intentionally creates no new supernatural power. Every effect derives from naming,
   writing, bodily witness, standing, office, threshold, refusal, or the collision between the two
   accepted Records.
8. The constitutional Naming section deliberately removes Naming from Clause Three. During manuscript
   repair, the Episode One language presently read as “Clause the third—the obligation shall be satisfied
