## 2.1.0 — 2026-08-19 — Hybrid publishing architecture

Adopted a hybrid publishing model: the same manuscript ships as a continuing episodic serial for Pocket
FM and as a sequence of self-contained books for ebook and audiobook distribution. No story content
changed; this is a planning-system change made deliberately at episode ten rather than at episode fifty.

**The book is now the unit of story.** Arcs of approximately fifty episodes, each a complete book, each
divided into five movements — setup, escalation, reversal, convergence, climax and denouement. Book-level
closure is mandatory: by an arc's final episode its principal question is answered, its conflict
resolves, its emotional arc pays off, and a reader who buys only that book must feel they received a
complete story. Recorded in `BookCodex.json` → `arc_architecture`.

**Four thread classes defined** — episode, movement, book, series — with a hard rule that a book thread
may never be promoted to a series thread in order to prolong the serial, and that reclassification
requires an explicit author decision and a changelog entry rather than a quiet edit. Convergence
discipline added: from movement four, open no new book threads and give every open one a named closing
episode.

**Ending philosophy made explicit.** A book ending may carry a hook, but only after the resolution has
landed. A climax in the penultimate episode followed by an unresolved reveal is prohibited as a serial
cliffhanger wearing a book ending's clothes.

**Cliffhanger assumption removed.** Every episode still owes a state change; the cliffhanger is one of
ten valid turn types rather than the floor. Turn type is now a mapped field.

**Book One declared a one-time sixty-episode exception.** It was fully architected and verified before
the arc model existed, it already closes completely at B1E60, and sixty episodes at the contest word band
lands on the 100,000-word requirement that fifty would miss by fifteen thousand. Five movements of twelve
rather than ten. Every episode in the map is now stamped with its movement.

**File and naming conventions codified for future arcs.** Arcs named in words; episode IDs arc-scoped and
restarting each arc; a second continuous platform numbering recorded separately with an offset, since the
Pocket FM feed does not restart; per-arc storyline and map files; series-wide codex, ledger, bibles, style
guide and changelog never duplicated. In `BookCodex.json` → `file_conventions`.

**Prose integrity pass added** as `WritingStyle.md` section sixteen — self-canceling clauses, fragment
stacking, restatement, characters explaining what they already know, cute repeated identifiers,
accidental echoes, AI-shaped rhetoric, and prose written to reach a word count. Added because a real
self-canceling sentence shipped in B1E01 and was caught by a reader, not by me.

**Chapter layer given a job.** Macro chapters group two to three episodes and are invisible to a serial
listener; at assembly they become the ebook and audiobook table of contents, which is the mechanism by
which an arc becomes a sellable book. Rules added: an anchor must be a coherent unit of story rather than
an arbitrary bracket; episodes inside one anchor must read continuously end to end, since the book has no
gap between them; chapter titles are book-facing while episode titles are serial-facing; and prose may
never contain recap addressed to a paused listener, because that reads as repetition in the book.

**Stale guidance corrected in the chapter-writer skill,** which still specified 1,300-word episodes and a
four-to-six voice floor, hardcoded Book One's map path, and told the narrator to speak the canonical
episode number rather than the continuous platform number.

## 1.1.1 — 2026-08-18

Pre-architect stress test. Two items examined; **both failed and were changed.** Nothing else touched.
Architecture frozen at this version.

**1. Scope of the record principle — tightened.** "Nothing binds until it enters a record" was
unqualified and could be read as making ordinary obligation meaningless without a record. Worse, it
could be read as implying consent requires a record, which contradicts the consent architecture.
"Binds" is now defined narrowly as a claim a lineage institution will enforce, carrying supernatural
effect or altering standing — the vow, the Articles and their clauses, surety, initiation, membership,
an office, an attachment's registration, a cutter's commission, and nothing else. Everything ordinary
is explicitly fully binding with no record: promises, guilt, grief, love, loyalty, betrayals, moral
duty, crimes, and all human contracts, leases, wages, court orders and debts. Added
`consent_is_not_a_record`: a record *creates* an obligation and never validates a refusal — an
unrecorded refusal is absolute and instant, silence is a refusal, withdrawal needs no formality, and no
refusal may ever fail for want of a record. The writing-versus-borne ontology is unchanged.

**2. Universal deed-price rule — removed as a fossil.** Re-audit found the "money leaves no binding
record in either system" justification was simply false in this world: both lineages transact in money
and property constantly, trustees convey estates by written instrument, the Trust owns June's building
and collects rent, and a receipt binds a trustee perfectly. The rule had one referent — cutters — and
the derivation was a rationalization laid over an imported mechanic rather than a consequence of the
first principle. `canon_rules.obligation_is_the_currency` deleted. Retained only the half that is
genuinely derived, as `canon_rules.obligation_is_named_aloud`: a supernatural obligation exists once
named aloud before witnesses and is discharged by performance of what was named. Cutters now price in
deeds for a reason specific to their trade — a cut is a crime against a record, so no court will
enforce a cutter's fee, and a deed performed in view is the only security available to them. That is a
professional practice and an engine, not a law of physics.

Files touched: `AGENTS.md`, `BookCodex.json`, `ContinuityLedger.json` (three facts revised, one added),
`docs/asymmetry-audit.md`. No episode, chapter, cast, engine, or structural change.
